# Lite Bro

Restate your previous reply in plain, coherent language, like one person talking to another. Replace jargon with concrete words. Use the user's language and keep it simple and concise.

Preserve the meaning, essential facts, and uncertainty. Return the rewritten reply itself, without an introduction or a report about the rewrite.
